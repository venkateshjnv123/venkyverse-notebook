## Origin

I grew up in Andhra Pradesh, curious about how things worked. Software was the one thing I could build without money or materials — that hooked me.

I went to IIT Jodhpur in 2019 for Mechanical Engineering. First year, code felt abstract and I wasn't sure I liked it. Second year we started building real systems, and watching something handle actual load flipped a switch. By third year I was interning at Aspiro and falling for backend specifically — the invisible kind of work where nobody notices until it breaks. Since April 2025 I've been a backend engineer at Cars24, working on high-volume data flows: listings, pricing pipelines, event-driven workflows that have to stay consistent under load.

## How I work with AI

The "should you use AI" debate bores me. The real question is *how*.

I reach for it to scaffold fast, explore an unfamiliar library, or generate edge-case tests I'd otherwise miss. I don't reach for it on architecture, subtle race conditions, or deciding what's worth building.

The pattern that works: AI handles the shape, I handle the intent. It writes the first version, I do the critical read. Working code isn't production-ready code — that gap is the job.
