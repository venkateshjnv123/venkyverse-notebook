Now I will never confuse TCP and UDP.

![TCP and UDP delivery behaviour explained with a meme](/images/036-tcp-vs-udp.jpg)

## TCP

- Both sides coordinate.
- The receiver confirms every packet.
- Failed? Retry.
- Slower, but safe.

## UDP

- Send it as fast as possible.
- No confirmation.
- No retries.
- Lower latency.

Real-world examples:

- **TCP:** payments, file transfers, API calls
- **UDP:** live video, gaming, DNS lookups

When you absolutely cannot lose data, reach for TCP. When speed matters more than perfect delivery, UDP may be the better fit.
