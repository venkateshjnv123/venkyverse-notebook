Interview preparation as a software engineer:

![The weekly DSA interview-preparation loop](/images/043-dsa-prep-loop.jpg)

**Monday:** Easy problem ✅<br />
“I am back.”

**Tuesday:** Medium problem ✅<br />
“Consistency is the key.”

**Wednesday:** Hard problem 💀<br />
“Kal bina solution dekh ke solve karunga?”

**Thursday:**<br />
Opens LeetCode.<br />
Stares at yesterday's hard problem for 20 minutes.<br />
Closes LeetCode.

**Friday:**<br />
“Ab toh weekend aa gaya… next Monday se properly start karte hain.”

**Saturday:** Enjoy.<br />
**Sunday:** Enjoy + slight guilt.

**Next Monday:** Easy problem ✅<br />
“Streak maintain karunga ab toh.”

And the loop continues until the interview invite arrives. 💀
