Custom notifications? No.

**Uber predictive notifications.** 💀

- **10:15 AM** → "Ready to ride to office?"
- **6:45 PM** → "Ready to ride back home?"

I didn't open the app. Didn't book anything. It just... knew.

![Uber predictive notifications — Mon 10:15 AM and Tue 6:45 PM](/images/uber-predictive-notifications.png)

Uber had already figured out:
- when I usually leave for office
- when I leave back home
- estimated arrival time at the destination
- current traffic conditions

All before I even unlocked my phone.

## What's likely happening under the hood

This isn't magic — it's a fairly elegant combination of systems:

**Pattern recognition** — Uber builds a personal routine model from your ride history. Same pickup zone, same time window, repeated across days = detected habit.

**Time-series ML** — a model scores the probability that you'll request a ride in the next ~15 minutes, triggered by time-of-day + day-of-week + your historical cadence.

**Real-time traffic** — once the prediction fires, it pulls live ETA data and stitches it into the notification copy before pushing.

**Push at the right moment** — not too early (you might ignore it), not too late (you've already left). The timing itself is a model output.

The result feels personal. Because it is — it's a model built entirely around *your* behaviour, not a generic broadcast.

Personalisation + predictive systems done right don't feel like a feature. They feel like the app just *gets* you. 🤯
